## Conventions I used
- Red only for initial th words (that, that's, the, there, this, throat, thank, three) and initial v (very).
  Final/medial v or th → yellow with a mouth note (have, give, level, fever, month, together, altogether).
- Words where the letter is silent say so in the note: receipt (p), sign (g), write/writing (w), should (l), Wednesday (d), talks (l).
- r-initial words are yellow (瑞/赖 + "嘴巴圆，舌头不碰上颚"); rice/right warn against l.
- s+p/t clusters: note that p/t become unaspirated (斯必 / 斯道).
